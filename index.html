Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 2026

Your privacy is fundamental to TrackX. We build tools that help you reflect on your life, and we believe your personal cadence belongs to you.

Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and authentication identifiers provided through Google or Firebase Authentication. When you use TrackX, your habits, check-ins, focus minutes, and wellness logs are stored to provide the tracking features.

How Your Data is Used

Your data is used solely to render your personal dashboard, calculate your streaks, and sync across your devices. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data or habits to any third party.

Data Storage & Security

Cloud data is encrypted in transit and at rest using Google Cloud Firestore and Firebase security rules. You can export or delete your data at any time from your Settings panel.
